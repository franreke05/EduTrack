## 2. What Data We Collect and Why

We apply the principle of data minimisation: we only process the data necessary for the App to function.

| Data category | Specific data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Account data** | Email address, username, and password (the password is managed and encrypted by Firebase Authentication; we do not store it or have access to it) | Creating and authenticating your account, enabling login and account recovery |
| **Profile data** | Username, active academic year, account settings | Personalising your in-app experience |
| **Academic data** | Subjects, grades, exams, academic years, calculated averages | Providing the core functionality: grade management, average calculation, and grade simulator |
| **Class group data** | Group membership, role (teacher/student), shared subjects and resources, grades shared within the group, group feed posts | Enabling collaboration between teachers and students within a group |
| **Subscription status** | Indicator of whether your account is premium (premiumCache) | Enabling or disabling premium features. We do **not** process payment data — this is handled exclusively by Google Play |
| **Advertising identifier** | Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID), processed via Google AdMob | Displaying advertising. For users identified as minors, we display **non-personalised advertising only** (see Section 8) |
| **Reminders** | Dates and details of your exams | Generating local reminder notifications on your device. These notifications are processed on-device |

We do not collect precise geolocation data, contacts, or special categories of personal data (Article 9 GDPR).

## 7. Minors and Parental Consent

EduTrack is aimed at students, including **minors between the ages of 13 and 17**.

- In Spain, pursuant to **Article 7 of the LOPDGDD**, minors under the age of **14** cannot give consent to the processing of their data on their own: the **consent of the holders of parental authority or guardianship** is required. This provision applies specifically to users in Spain under the LOPDGDD.
- In other EU member states, the GDPR allows this age threshold to be set at up to **16 years** (Article 8 GDPR). Users in other jurisdictions should refer to the applicable local law.

Therefore:

1. During registration we request the user's **age or date of birth** (age gate).
2. If the user is below the applicable digital consent age, **verifiable consent from a parent or guardian** is required before proceeding.
3. All users identified as minors are subject to the minor-specific processing: **non-personalised advertising** and restriction of inappropriate features.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided data without adequate consent, please write to us at **franreke506@gmail.com** and we will delete the data.
