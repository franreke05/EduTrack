## 2. What data we collect and why

We follow the principle of data minimisation: we only process the data needed for the App to work.

| Data category | Specific data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Account data** | Email address, username and password (the password is managed and encrypted by Firebase Authentication; we never store or see it) | Create and authenticate your account, enable sign-in and password recovery |
| **Profile data** | Username, active academic year, account settings | Personalise your experience in the App |
| **Academic data** | Subjects, grades, exams, academic years, calculated averages | Provide the core features: grade management, average calculation and the grade simulator |
| **Class group data** | Group membership, role (teacher/student), shared subjects and resources, grades shared within a group, group feed posts | Enable collaboration between teachers and students within a group |
| **Subscription status** | A flag indicating whether your account is premium (premiumCache) | Enable or disable premium features. We do **not** process payment data — this is handled exclusively by Google Play |
| **Advertising identifier** | Device Advertising ID, processed through Google AdMob | Show advertising. For users who are minors we show **non-personalised advertising only** (see section 8) |
| **Reminders** | Your exam dates and details | Generate local reminder notifications on your device. These notifications are processed on-device |

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR).

## 7. Minors and parental consent

EduTrack is aimed at students, including **minors aged 13 to 17**.

- In Spain, under **Art. 7 LOPDGDD**, minors under **14** cannot provide consent themselves to the processing of their data: **consent from a parent or legal guardian** is required.
- In other EU countries, the age of digital consent may be set up to **16** (Art. 8 GDPR).

Therefore:

1. During registration we request the user's **age or date of birth** (age-gate).
2. If the user is below the applicable age of digital consent, **verifiable consent from a parent or guardian** is required before continuing.
3. All users identified as minors receive the child-directed treatment: **non-personalised advertising** and restriction of features that are not appropriate.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided data without appropriate consent, email **franreke506@gmail.com** and we will delete that data.
